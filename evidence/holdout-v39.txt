HONESTY ABOUT THE PROCESS — what re-verification caught
================================================================
1. The gauntlet re-run caught a real, rare defect: the boot
   preflight's writable probe used a FIXED path — two simultaneous
   boots could unlink each other's probe and misreport a WRITABLE
   workspace as unwritable (the 1-in-N rc=2 in the concurrent-boot
   group). Reproduced deterministically: 51/320 misreports under a
   threaded hammer. Fixed with a unique auto-cleaning probe: 0/320.
   Regression-tested permanently in the suite.
2. The first wheel verification run FAILED on the wheel but passed
   on the host — because the wheel had been built before the last
   fix landed (missing-workspace guard). Caught by exactly the
   verify-the-wheel law this project adopted at v39.3; the wheel
   was rebuilt from the final tree and everything re-run green.
   Built is not tested.
3. A missing --workspace tracebacks raw on the first pass — now a
   named refusal with remedy. The plain-language law extends to
   CLI edges.
